Tournament Schedule

Friday, May 22 — Skins Game

Optional skins game to kick off the weekend. A great warm-up round and a chance to win some cash before the main event.

Saturday, May 23 — Round 1 & Calcutta

Round 1: First 18 holes of stroke play competition. Flights determined.

Calcutta Auction: After Round 1, players are auctioned off to bidders. Top 3 finishers per flight pay out. You can buy yourself back if you're outbid.

Sunday, May 24 — Round 2 & Awards

Round 2: Final 18 holes. Scores from both rounds determine flight winners.

Awards Ceremony: Flight winners announced, scholarships awarded, raffle drawing, and Calcutta payouts.

What Is a Calcutta?

A Calcutta is a golf auction pool — part competition, part entertainment. After Saturday's Round 1, every player in the tournament is "auctioned off" to the highest bidder. If you believe a player is going to finish strong on Sunday, you bid on them.

The top 3 finishers in each flight pay out to whoever "owns" them. Here's the twist: if someone bids on you and you don't want them to profit from your game, you can buy yourself back. It adds a layer of strategy, fun, and friendly rivalry to the tournament.

The Calcutta is one of the most anticipated traditions of the Cleve Austin tournament — part poker night, part pep rally, all fun.
