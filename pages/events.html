Memorial Day Tournament

Completed

May 22–24, 2026

Format: 36-hole stroke play • Entry: $180 • Open to all

The 37th Annual Cleve Austin Memorial Day Scholarship Tournament — the club's signature event. Three days of competition at Ross Rogers Golf Complex featuring six flights, a Friday skins game, Saturday Calcutta, and Sunday awards.

Partnership

Completed

July 11, 2026

Format: 2-Person Scramble + Modified Chapman • Open to all

Played Saturday, July 11 at Ross Rogers — 34 teams competed. Flight winners: Rudy Ibarra & Brennan Byrd (1st Flight, 62), Ace Garcia & Omar Ramos (2nd Flight, 68), and Zak Roberts & Jeremiah Pena (3rd Flight, 72). Thank you to every team who came out to support the scholarship fund.

Club Championship

Completed

Monday, September 7, 2026 — Labor Day

Format: Stroke play • Members only

Played Monday, September 7 at Ross Rogers — 27 members competed. 2026 Club Champion: David Hopper (72, even par); runner-up Rudy Ibarra (74). Net division: Mal Carr (69), Jeremy Dockery (70), Matt Loftin (70).

Ryder Cup Matches

Confirmed

Saturday, October 17, 2026

Format: Two-person teams, team match play • Open to all — members and non-members

Entry: Fee to be announced • Entries close Monday, October 12 • Field limited to 24 teams, accepted in the order received

The club's own Ryder Cup — team match play competition that caps the season with camaraderie and competitive fire. Teams are drafted and matches played in the spirit of the original Ryder Cup.
