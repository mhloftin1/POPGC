Membership

Join the Pride of the Panhandle Golf Club

What It Means to Be a Member

The Pride of the Panhandle Golf Club is a chapter of the Texas State Golf Association — one of 13 across the state. Membership means being part of a community of golfers who share a love for the game and a commitment to investing in the next generation through scholarships.

Members have access to the full club calendar, including the members-only Club Championship. Most club events are open to non-members as well, but membership is where the real sense of belonging lives — regular rounds, shared competition, and lifelong friendships built on the course.

Member Benefits

🏆

Club Championship

Compete in the annual members-only Club Championship — stroke play format to determine the year's club champion.

🤝

Fellowship

Regular rounds and events with a group of golfers who've been playing together for decades. New members are welcomed warmly.

🌎

TSGA Network

Access to the broader Texas State Golf Association tournament circuit — competitive play across all 13 chapters statewide.
