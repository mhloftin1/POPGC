Our Mission

The Pride of the Panhandle Golf Club awards scholarships to students across the Texas Panhandle — $500 per student, paid directly to the institution upon proof of enrollment. Every entry fee, every sponsorship, every raffle ticket supports the next generation.

A Championship Venue

Ross Rogers Golf Complex — ranked the #1 economy course in Texas by the Dallas Morning News — hosts our tournament May 22–24, weeks before welcoming the Korn Ferry Tour for its Monday Qualifier.
